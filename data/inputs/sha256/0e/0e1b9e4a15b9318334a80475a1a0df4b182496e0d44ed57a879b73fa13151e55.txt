**Add a Pinch of Spice: Lemon Zest and Grated Parmesan**

Sprinkle a small amount of grated Parmesan cheese and a pinch of lemon zest over your freshly popped popcorn. The combination will add a surprising depth of flavor and aroma. The nutty sweetness of the popcorn pairs perfectly with the salty, umami flavor of Parmesan, while the lemon zest provides a bright, citrusy note.

Tips:

* Use freshly grated Parmesan for the best flavor.
* Start with a small amount (about 1/4 teaspoon Parmesan and a pinch of lemon zest per 1/2 cup popcorn) and adjust to taste.
* You can also add a drizzle of olive oil if you prefer a richer flavor profile.

This hack is a game-changer for movie nights, parties, or a quick snack attack! Enjoy!
