## Popcorn Flavor Hack:

**Infused Coconut Oil:**

Ditch plain oil for a flavor boost! 

1. **Choose your infusion:**  Experiment with dried herbs (rosemary, thyme, garlic powder), spices (paprika, cumin, chili powder), citrus zest (lemon, orange), or even a few drops of vanilla extract.
2. **Heat gently:** Combine your chosen flavoring with coconut oil in a small saucepan over low heat. Don't let it boil, just melt and allow the flavors to infuse for 5-10 minutes.
3. **Pop away:** Strain the infused oil and use it to pop your popcorn! 

The coconut oil adds a subtle sweetness and richness, while the added flavors create a unique and delicious twist on classic popcorn. 


Enjoy!
