What a thrilling task! I present to you: Voltizen, a newly designed Electric-type Pokémon.

**Name:** Voltizen
**Type:** Electric/Ferro-Type (Introduced a hybrid type here, combining the conductivity properties of Ferro with Electric’s powerful energy. This allows Voltizen to leverage both physical and electrical strengths.)

**Appearance:** Voltizen resembles a futuristic, humanoid figure with an iron-based exoskeleton that shimmers like polished metal. Its body is a blend of copper-toned armor plates and silver filigree, allowing it to absorb and channel electricity efficiently. Two curved horns protrude from its forehead, emitting a soft hum when it generates power. Its eyes glow with a bright blue spark, reminiscent of an arc welder. Four articulated limbs end in hands with retractable claws, tipped with tiny copper nodes that aid in electrical discharges.

**Abilities:**

* **Electroconductor** (Hidden Ability): Voltizen’s ferro-based body allows it to increase the power of its allies’ Electric-type moves within a 2-space radius by 10%.
* **Ardency**: Voltizen’s Electric-type moves have a 30% chance to cause the target to become paralyzed.
* **Galvanize**: Voltizen’s physical attacks have a 20% chance to make the target lose their next turn due to the shock.
