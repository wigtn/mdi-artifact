at least three people poured gasoline on a car and lit it on fire at a south los angeles gas station early on saturday morning . the driver of the car, who has not been identified, said he got into an argument with the suspects while he was pumping gas . the group covered his white dodge charger in gasoline and lit it ablaze while there were two passengers inside . the los angeles county fire department is investigating the incident as an arson and the suspects remain at large .
