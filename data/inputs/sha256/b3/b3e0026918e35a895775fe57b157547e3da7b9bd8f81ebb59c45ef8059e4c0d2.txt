The classic delight - Chewy Chocolate Chip Cookies! Here's a step-by-step guide to prepare these soft and scrumptious treats:

**Ingredients:**

* 2 1/4 cups All-Purpose Flour
* 1 tsp Baking Soda
* 1 tsp Salt
* 1 cup (2 sticks) Unsalted Butter, at room temperature
* 3/4 cup White Granulated Sugar
* 3/4 cup Brown Sugar
* 2 large Eggs
* 2 teaspoons Vanilla Extract
* 2 cups Semi-Sweet Chocolate Chips

**Preparation Steps:**

1. **Preheat**: Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper or a silicone mat to ensure easy cookie removal.
2. **Whisk Dry Ingredients**: In a medium bowl, whisk together flour, baking soda, and salt. Set aside.
3. **Cream Butter and Sugars**: In a large bowl, use an electric mixer to cream together the butter and sugars until light and fluffy, about 2-3 minutes.
4. **Add Eggs and Vanilla**: Beat in the eggs one at a time, followed by the vanilla extract, until well combined.
5. **Mix Dry Ingredients**: Gradually mix the dry ingredients (flour mixture) into the wet ingredients until a dough forms. Be gentle to avoid overmixing.
6. **Chocolate Chip Magic**: Stir in those delicious chocolate chips.
7. **Scoop and Space**: Use a cookie scoop or tablespoon to drop rounded balls of dough onto the prepared baking sheet, leaving about 2 inches of space between each cookie to allow for spreading.
8. **Bake**: Bake for 10-12 minutes or until the edges are lightly golden brown and the centers are set. Avoid overbaking for a chewy texture.
9. **Cool**: Remove the cookies from the oven and let them cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes before transferring them to a wire rack to cool completely.

Enjoy your chewy, chocolatey goodness!
