## Subtopics for Music Theory:

**Fundamentals:**

* **Rhythm and Meter:**
    * Note values and durations
    * Time signatures
    * Tempo and meter markings
    * Polyrhythms
    * Syncopation
* **Melody and Pitch:**
    * Scales and keys
    * Intervals and chord construction
    * Pitch notation (treble and bass clef)
    * Melodic contour and phrasing
* **Harmony and Chords:**
    * Triads and inversions
    * Seventh chords and extensions
    * Chord progressions
    * Cadences and harmonic analysis
* **Form and Structure:**
    * Binary, ternary, and sonata form
    * Theme and variations
    * Rondo form
    * Through-composed form

**Advanced Concepts:**

* **Counterpoint:**
    * Two-part and multi-part counterpoint
    * Imitation and canon
    * Fugue
* **Voice Leading:**
    * Smooth and logical voice movement
    * Consonance and dissonance
    * Common tones and neighbor tones
* **Modulation and Key Changes:**
    * Secondary dominants
    * Pivot chords
    * Modulation techniques
* **Analysis and Interpretation:**
    * Identifying musical elements in a score
    * Understanding composer's intent
    * Explaining musical structure and meaning

**Genres and Styles:**

* **Western Classical Music:** Baroque, Classical, Romantic, 20th Century
* **Jazz Theory:**
    * Blues scales and progressions
    * Swing rhythm
    * Improvisation
* **Popular Music Theory:**
    * Pop song structure
    * Chord voicings and inversions
    * Rhythm and groove

**Tools and Resources:**

* **Music notation software:** Finale, Sibelius, MuseScore
* **Ear training apps:** Tenuto, Functional Ear Trainer
* **Online music theory websites:** musictheory.net, Teoria.com
* **Books and textbooks:** The Complete Idiot's Guide to Music Theory, Harmony by Walter Piston


This list is not exhaustive, but it provides a comprehensive overview of the various subtopics within music theory.
