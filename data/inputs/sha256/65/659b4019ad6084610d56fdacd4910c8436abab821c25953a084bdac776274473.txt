The ESRB rating for this action-adventure game is M – Mature. This rating is assigned due to the following factors:

* Blood and gore: The game features large blood-splatter effects, dismemberment, impalement, and decapitation during combat.
* Strong Language: The game includes the use of the words “f**k” and “sh*t”.
* Violence: Frequent intense action-based combat against various enemies involving hand-to-hand combat, bladed weapons, and blunt weapons like axes.

The ESRB summary for this rating is as follows: “Blood and gore, Strong Language, Violence.”
