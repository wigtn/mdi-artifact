Great! A road trip from San Diego to Los Angeles along the beautiful California coast is an excellent choice. Here's a suggested itinerary with some stops and activities along the way:

Day 1: San Diego
- Start your trip by exploring San Diego's famous Balboa Park
- Visit the San Diego Zoo
- Enjoy dinner at the Gaslamp Quarter

Day 2: San Diego to Carlsbad (35 miles)
- Morning at La Jolla Cove for beach time and seal watching
- Stop at Torrey Pines State Natural Reserve for hiking
- Visit LEGOLAND California in Carlsbad
- Overnight in Carlsbad

Day 3: Carlsbad to Laguna Beach (60 miles)
- Morning at Carlsbad State Beach
- Visit Mission San Juan Capistrano
- Explore Laguna Beach's art galleries and beaches
- Dinner at one of Laguna Beach's oceanfront restaurants

Day 4: Laguna Beach to Long Beach (40 miles)
- Drive through Newport Beach and stop at Balboa Island
- Visit the Aquarium of the Pacific in Long Beach
- Tour the Queen Mary
- Overnight in Long Beach

Day 5: Long Beach to Los Angeles (25 miles)
- Start early and head to Los Angeles
- Visit the Getty Center for art and panoramic views
- Explore Santa Monica Pier and Venice Beach
- End your trip with dinner in downtown LA or Hollywood

This itinerary offers a mix of natural beauty, cultural attractions, and iconic California experiences. You can adjust the pace based on your preferences, spending more or less time at each location. The total driving distance is about 160 miles, allowing for a leisurely trip with plenty of time to explore each stop.
