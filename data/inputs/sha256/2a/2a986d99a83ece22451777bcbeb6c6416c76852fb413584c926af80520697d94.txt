## San Diego to Los Angeles: A Coastal Cruiser Road Trip

This road trip focuses on the stunning Pacific coastline, with a mix of beach vibes, charming towns, and iconic LA attractions.

**Day 1: San Diego Sun & Surfing**

* **Morning:** Start your journey in sunny San Diego! Explore the vibrant **Gaslamp Quarter**, with its historic architecture and trendy shops. Grab brunch at **Snooze, an A.M. Eatery** for creative pancakes and coffee.
* **Afternoon:** Hit the iconic **Coronado Beach**, known for its soft sands and charming Hotel del Coronado. Rent bikes and cruise along the boardwalk, or try stand-up paddleboarding.
* **Evening:**  Catch a sunset at **La Jolla Cove**, a picturesque spot teeming with seals and sea lions. Enjoy dinner at **George's at the Cove** for oceanfront dining and breathtaking views.

**Day 2: Coastal Charm & Wine Country**

* **Morning:** Drive north along the scenic **Pacific Coast Highway (PCH)**, stopping at **Torrey Pines State Natural Reserve** for stunning coastal views and hiking trails.
* **Afternoon:** Explore the charming town of **Encinitas**, known for its bohemian vibe and art scene. Browse the shops on **Main Street** and grab lunch at **The Cottage**, a local favorite.
* **Evening:** Venture inland to **Temecula Valley Wine Country**. Enjoy a wine tasting at **Wilson Creek Winery** and savor a farm-to-table dinner at **The Restaurant at Ponte Winery**.

**Day 3: Hollywood Glamour & Beachside Bliss**

* **Morning:** Continue north on the PCH, stopping at **Laguna Beach** for its art galleries, tide pools, and breathtaking beaches. Hike to the top of **Heisler Park** for panoramic ocean views.
* **Afternoon:** Arrive in Los Angeles and check into your hotel. Head to **Santa Monica Pier** for amusement park rides, street performers, and iconic Pacific Ocean views.
* **Evening:**  Experience the magic of **Hollywood Boulevard**. Walk the Walk of Fame, visit the TCL Chinese Theatre, and catch a show at the legendary **Dolby Theatre**.

**Bonus Stops & Activities:**

* **Whale Watching Tour (San Diego):** Spot migrating gray whales during their annual journey.
* **LEGOLAND California (Carlsbad):** Fun for families with LEGO-themed rides and attractions.
* **Huntington Library, Art Collections, and Botanical Gardens (San Marino):**  A cultural oasis with stunning gardens and art exhibits.
* **Griffith Observatory (Los Angeles):** Enjoy panoramic city views and explore space exhibits.
* **Venice Beach Boardwalk (Los Angeles):**  A vibrant and eclectic mix of street performers, artists, and shops.

**Remember to:**

* Book accommodations in advance, especially during peak season.
* Pack layers as the weather can be unpredictable along the coast.
* Allow ample time for driving, as traffic can be heavy in LA.
* Enjoy the journey! This road trip is about savoring the beauty of the California coastline and creating lasting memories.
