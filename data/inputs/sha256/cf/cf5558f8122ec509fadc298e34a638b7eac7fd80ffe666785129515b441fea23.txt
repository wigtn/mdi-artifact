residents on the east coast of japan 's hokkaido island had an unexpected change of scenery this morning after 1,000 ft of seabed was forced to the surface overnight . the extra stretch of coastline on shiretoko peninsula near the town of rausu has risen as high as 50ft from the sea surface in some places , exposing what used to be the ocean floor . geologists believe the emergence is a result of a landslide nearby , when melting ice and snow caused a section of land to drop , pivoting the underwater area into the air .
