Here are some mottos for your quaint ice cream shop, playing on its location, variety, and loyalty program:

**Short & Sweet:**

* **Historic Flavors, Sweet Memories.**
* **Scoops of History, Chunks of Delight.**
* **Your Historic District Sweet Spot.**
* **Where History Meets Happiness (in a Cone).**

**Highlighting Variety:**

* **Ice Cream, Gelato, Sorbet: Your Frozen Fantasy Awaits.**
* **A World of Flavors, One Scoop at a Time.**
* **Beyond Ice Cream: Discover Your New Favorite Treat.**

**Loyalty Focused:**

* **10 Scoops to Sweet Rewards.** (Directly mentions the program)
* **Taste the Tradition, Earn Your Free Scoop.**
* **Sweeten Your History, Join Our Scoop Club.**

**Tips for Choosing:**

* **Keep it concise and memorable.**
* **Reflect the shop's unique charm and location.**
* **Consider your target audience (families, locals, tourists).**
* **Test it out!** Say it aloud, see how it looks on signage, get feedback.


Good luck finding the perfect motto!
