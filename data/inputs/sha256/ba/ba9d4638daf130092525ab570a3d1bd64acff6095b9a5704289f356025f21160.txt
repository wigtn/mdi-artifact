A thrilling genre! Here are some popular Crime TV Shows:

1. **Narcos** (2015-2017) - a biographical crime drama about Pablo Escobar and the Medellín cartel.
2. **Breaking Bad** (2008-2013) - a story of a high school teacher turned meth kingpin.
3. **True Detective** (2014-present) - an anthology series featuring self-contained cases and complex characters.
4. **Sherlock** (2010-present) - a modern retelling of Sir Arthur Conan Doyle's classic detective stories.
5. **Peaky Blinders** (2013-present) - a historical crime drama set in post-WW1 England.
6. **Homeland** (2011-2020) - a CIA agent investigates terrorist plots in this gripping spy-thriller.
7. **Bones** (2005-2017) - a forensic anthropology crime drama.
8. **Law & Order: SVU** (1999-present) - a long-running series focusing on sexual offenses and investigations.
9. **Dexter** (2006-2013) - a vigilante thriller about a serial killer who targets criminals.
10. **Mindhunter** (2017-2019) - set in the late 1970s, FBI agents hunt serial killers in this critically acclaimed series.

These shows will keep you on the edge of your seat and addicted to the crime-solving action!
