Here's a list of subtopics for Music Theory:

1. Pitch and Scales
   - Major and minor scales
   - Modes
   - Chromatic scale

2. Rhythm and Meter
   - Time signatures
   - Tempo
   - Syncopation

3. Harmony
   - Chord construction
   - Chord progressions
   - Cadences

4. Melody
   - Melodic intervals
   - Phrase structure
   - Motifs and themes

5. Form and Structure
   - Song forms (e.g., AABA, verse-chorus)
   - Classical forms (e.g., sonata, rondo)

6. Notation
   - Staff notation
   - Clefs
   - Key signatures

7. Ear Training
   - Interval recognition
   - Chord identification
   - Melodic dictation

8. Counterpoint
   - Two-part writing
   - Fugue

9. Orchestration
   - Instrument ranges
   - Timbre and texture
   - Arranging techniques

10. Music Analysis
    - Roman numeral analysis
    - Schenkerian analysis

11. Composition Techniques
    - Modulation
    - Transposition
    - Variation

12. Music Technology
    - MIDI
    - Digital audio workstations (DAWs)
    - Sound synthesis

13. Historical Music Theory
    - Medieval and Renaissance theory
    - Baroque theory
    - 20th-century techniques

14. Jazz Theory
    - Chord extensions
    - Improvisation
    - Reharmonization

15. World Music Theory
    - Non-Western scales and modes
    - Rhythmic cycles
    - Microtonal systems
