This paper introduces FewJoint, a first-of-its-kind few-shot learning (FSL) benchmark for joint dialogue understanding, comprising 59 dialogue domains from real industrial sources. To address the challenges of FSL in joint intent detection and slot filling, the proposed method, which includes a trust gating mechanism to filter noisy intent information and a Reptile-based meta-learning strategy, achieves significant improvements and sets a new state-of-the-art performance on two datasets, advancing research in few-shot dialogue understanding.
