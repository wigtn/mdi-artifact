harry kane is nominated for both the pfa player and young player of the year . tottenham striker features in a clever new animation, which features some of his best goals, and celebrations from the season . the video, produced by copa90, uses still photographs of the england forward placed one on top of the other to produce an animated effect . it features his long-range strike against chelsea, a headed goal against arsenal, and the moment he came on - and scored - on his england debut .
