English constituency parsing is the task of analyzing a sentence and breaking it down into its constituent parts, such as phrases and clauses, and identifying the structure of those constituents (e.g., noun phrase, verb phrase, relative clause, etc.) within the sentence. It's a sub-task of natural language processing (NLP) that focuses on grammatical structure analysis. In this context, the goal is to predict the hierarchical structure of a sentence, given an input sentence as input.
