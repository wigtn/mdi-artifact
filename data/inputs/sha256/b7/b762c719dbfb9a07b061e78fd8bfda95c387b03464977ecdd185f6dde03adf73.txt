residents on the east coast of japan 's hokkaido island had an unexpected change of scenery this morning after 1,000ft of seabed was forced to the surface overnight . the extra stretch of coastline on shiretoko peninsula near the town of rausu has risen as high as 50ft from the sea surface in some places , exposing what used to be the ocean floor . the mass , which has risen some 30 to 50ft above sea level , measures roughly 1,000 - 1,640ft long , and 100ft wide , a town official said .
