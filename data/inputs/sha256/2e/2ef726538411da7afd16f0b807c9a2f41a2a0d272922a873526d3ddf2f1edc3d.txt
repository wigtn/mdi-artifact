Certainly! Here's a mnemonic for the Kanji meaning "Wish" using the primitives clock and heart:

"Time ticks by on the clock, but the heart's deepest wish is to make every moment count."

This mnemonic connects the idea of a wish or desire (represented by the heart) with the concept of time (represented by the clock). It emphasizes how our wishes are often related to time - either wanting more time for something we love, or wishing for a particular moment to arrive.

Alternatively, you could use:

"The heart's greatest wish is to turn back the clock on precious moments."

This version plays on the common wish to relive or extend cherished times in our lives, linking the heart (emotions and desires) with the clock (time).
