For this charming ice cream shop, I suggest the following motto:

"Scooping up history, one flavor at a time."

This motto captures several key aspects of the business:

1. It references the shop's location in the historic district, tying the brand to the area's heritage.
2. The word "scooping" directly relates to ice cream service.
3. "One flavor at a time" highlights the variety of flavors offered and suggests a personalized experience for each customer.
4. It has a playful and memorable quality that suits an ice cream shop.

This motto could be used in various marketing materials, such as signage, menus, social media posts, and loyalty cards. It effectively communicates the shop's unique selling points: its historic location, variety of flavors, and personalized service.
