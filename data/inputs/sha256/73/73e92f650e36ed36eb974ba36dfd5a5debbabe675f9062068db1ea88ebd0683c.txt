Here are 10 names from various cultures that evoke meanings related to love, care, and empathy:

1. **Ahava (Hebrew):**  Directly translates to "love."
2. **Carys (Welsh):** Means "love" and is associated with tenderness and affection.
3. **Prem (Sanskrit):**  Means "love" and is a common name in India, often associated with devotion and compassion.
4. **Amani (Arabic):** Means "wishes" or "aspirations," often interpreted as a desire for love, peace, and well-being.
5. **Zia (Italian):** Means "light" but also carries connotations of warmth, love, and guidance.
6. **Maeve (Irish):**  Means "intoxicating" or "she who rules," but also has associations with compassion and nurturing.
7. **Agape (Greek):** Refers to a selfless, unconditional love often associated with spiritual and philosophical concepts.
8. **Kuan (Chinese):** Means "love" or "kindness" and is often used in a benevolent context.
9. **Compassion (Various):** While not a traditional name, it directly conveys the meaning and has gained popularity as a unique choice.
10. **Amara (Igbo):** Means "grace" or "mercy," implying kindness and empathy.



Remember that the cultural context and personal interpretations can influence the nuances associated with each name.
