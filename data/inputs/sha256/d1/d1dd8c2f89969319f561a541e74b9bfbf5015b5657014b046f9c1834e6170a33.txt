Let's say Navina's sister has x dollars. Since Navina has 30 dollars more, Navina has x + 30 dollars. We know they add up to a total budget (since it's a shared budget context), so we can set up the equation: x + (x + 30) = total budget. We can simplify to 2x + 30. Now, we need to decide on the total budget (e.g., weekly budget for what, but let's assume a general weekly budget). Since there's no specified total, we'll use a reasonable value, 100 (dollars). 2x + 30 = 100 → 2x = 70 → x = 35. So, Navina's sister has 35 dollars, and Navina has 35 + 30 = 65 dollars. The answer is: Navina's sister: 35, Navina: 65 (until the credit card expense, that is!).
