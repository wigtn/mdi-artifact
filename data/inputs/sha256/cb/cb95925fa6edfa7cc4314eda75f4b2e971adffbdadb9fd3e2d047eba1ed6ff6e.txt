For this charming, multi-textured ice cream shop in the heart of the historic district, here’s a motto that will resonate with its customers and become a cornerstone of its marketing strategies:

**“Savor the Heritage, Collect the Joy.”**

This motto reflects the shop’s location in the historic district, connecting customers to the past. “Savor the Heritage” encourages customers to appreciate not only the rich history surrounding them but also the traditional techniques used to craft their unique Italian-inspired frozen treats.

The second part, “Collect the Joy,” ties in with the loyalty program, where customers earn a free scoop after 10 purchases. It conveys the idea that every visit to the shop brings happiness and that collecting those moments leads to a greater reward.

This motto can be used across various marketing platforms:

* Logo and branding
* Social media hashtags (#SavorTheHeritageCollectTheJoy)
* In-store signage and merchandise
* Loyalty program promotions
* Email newsletters and subscriptions
* Online ordering and delivery services

Overall, “Savor the Heritage, Collect the Joy” embodies the shop’s unique blend of history, quality products, and customer loyalty, making it an effective and memorable motto for their marketing strategies.
