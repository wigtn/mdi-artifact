Here are some highly acclaimed and popular Crime TV Shows across various years:

1. **Recent Hits:**
   - **Breaking Bad** (2008-2013): A story of a high school chemistry teacher turned meth kingpin and the agent determined to catch him.
   - **Narcos** (2015-2017): The rise and fall of Colombian drug kingpin Pablo Escobar and the DEA agents on his tail.
   - **True Detective** (2014-present): An anthology series featuring standalone seasons with complex, crime-solving narratives and philosophical undertones.
   - **Peaky Blinders** (2013-present): Set in post-World War I England, it revolves around a gangster family’s struggle for power and survival.
   - **Mindhunter** (2017-2019): Based on the true-crime book, FBI agents interview and profile serial killers in the late 1970s.

Classic Favorites:
   - **Law & Order: Special Victims Unit** (1999-present)
   - **The Sopranos** (1999-2007)
   - **24** (2001-2010, 2014)
   - **Homicide: Life on the Street** (1993-1999)
   - **Columbo** (1971-2003)

These TV shows have captivated audiences with their intricate plots, memorable characters, and social commentary. Their success has also led to numerous awards and critical acclaim.

As for movies in the same genre, here are some notable ones:
   - **Se7en** (1995): A dark and gritty thriller about two detectives hunting a serial killer who uses the seven deadly sins as a motif for his murders.
   - **Chinatown** (1974): A neo-noir classic about a private investigator uncovering corruption and deceit in 1930s Los Angeles.
   - **The Silence of the Lambs** (1991): An FBI trainee seeks the advice of imprisoned Dr. Hannibal Lecter to catch another serial killer.
   - **Memento** (2000): A reverse-told story about a man suffering from short-term memory loss seeking revenge for his wife’s murder.
   - **Zodiac** (2007): Based on a true story, it follows three investigators as they hunt for the Zodiac Killer in the 1970s.

These films have not only entertained but also explored the complexities of human nature and the darker aspects of society.
