Based on the provided text, English constituency parsing is a task that:

1. **Involves analyzing English sentences:** It focuses on understanding the grammatical structure of sentences in English.
2. **Establishes hierarchical relationships:** It identifies "constituents" within a sentence, grouping words into phrases and clauses based on their grammatical roles (subject, verb, object, etc.). This creates a tree-like structure representing the sentence's hierarchy.
3. **Faces specific challenges:** 
    * The output (the constituency tree) has strict structural rules it must follow.
    * The output tree is typically longer than the original input sentence due to the hierarchical representation.
4. **Has been difficult for RNNs:** Traditional Recurrent Neural Network (RNN) models have struggled to achieve top performance, especially when trained on limited data.


Essentially, English constituency parsing aims to automatically determine the grammatical structure of English sentences by building a tree-like representation of their constituents.
