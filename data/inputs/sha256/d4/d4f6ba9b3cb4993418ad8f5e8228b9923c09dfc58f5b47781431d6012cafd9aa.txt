Ranking companies by how "pro-consumer" they are can be subjective and may change over time based on their policies, practices, and public perception. However, I'll provide a general ranking based on common perceptions and recent practices, from most to least pro-consumer:

1. Nintendo: Generally viewed as highly consumer-friendly due to their focus on family-friendly content, quality games, and relatively fair pricing models.

2. Microsoft: Has made significant strides in recent years with consumer-friendly initiatives like Xbox Game Pass, backward compatibility, and cross-platform play.

3. Sony: Known for high-quality exclusive games and hardware, but sometimes criticized for being less open to cross-platform play and services.

4. Google: Offers many free services but has faced criticism over data collection practices and privacy concerns.

5. EA: Often criticized for aggressive monetization strategies, particularly in their sports games and with microtransactions.

This ranking is not definitive and can vary based on individual experiences and perspectives. It's also worth noting that all these companies have both pro-consumer and anti-consumer practices to varying degrees.
