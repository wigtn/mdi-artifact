As a software engineer, highlighting the right skills on LinkedIn can increase visibility, credibility, and career opportunities. Here's a curated list of desirable skills to add to your LinkedIn profile:
**Core Programming Skills:**

1. **Programming languages:**
	* Java, Python, JavaScript (with frameworks like React, Node.js)
	* C++, C#, Go
2. ** Familiarity with multiple paradigms:** Object-Oriented Programming (OOP), Functional Programming, and Scripting

**Development Frameworks and Technologies:**

3. **Web Development:**
	* Microservices Architecture
	* Spring Boot, Django, Flask
	* React, Angular, Vue.js
4. **Databases:**
	* Relational: MySQL, PostgreSQL
	* NoSQL: MongoDB, Cassandra, GraphQL
5. **Cloud Platforms:**
	* Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud Platform (GCP)

**Collaboration, Testing, and Deployment:**

6. **Agile Methodologies:** Scrum, Kanban
7. **Version Control:** Git, SVN
8. **Testing Frameworks:** JUnit, PyUnit, Jest, Cypress
9. **Continuous Integration/Continuous Deployment (CI/CD):** Jenkins, Travis CI, CircleCI

**Data Science and Analytics:**

10. **Data Science:** Familiarity with NumPy, Pandas, scikit-learn
11. **Machine Learning:** TensorFlow, PyTorch
12. **Data Visualization:** Tableau, D3.js

**Soft Skills and Tools:**

13. **Communication:** Effective communication of technical concepts
14. **Problem-Solving:** Proven problem-solving skills
15. **Team Collaboration:** Experience working in Agile teams
16. **Documentation:** Knowledge of documentation tools like Markdown, Doxygen, or Sphinx
17. **Issue Tracking:** Jira, Trello

**Additional Nice-to-Have Skills:**

18. **Security:** Security best practices, OAuth, Authorization
19. **DevOps:** Docker, Kubernetes
20. **Artificial Intelligence/Automation:** Automation testing, AI-powered development tools

Remember to:

* Only add skills you're proficient in or have experience with.
* Be specific when listing skills (e.g., "JavaScript (React)" instead of just "JavaScript").
* Keep your skill list relevant to your current role, target role, or industry.
* Regularly update your skills as you acquire new ones to demonstrate your growth and versatility.

By highlighting these skills on your LinkedIn profile, you'll increase your visibility, credibility, and attractiveness to potential employers and clients. Good luck!
