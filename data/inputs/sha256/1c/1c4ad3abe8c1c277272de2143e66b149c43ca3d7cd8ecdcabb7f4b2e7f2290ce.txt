As of 2023, the President of the Federal Republic of Germany is Frank-Walter Steinmeier. However, it’s important to note that in Germany’s political system, the Chancellor (currently Olaf Scholz) holds more executive powers than the President. The President serves as a ceremonial head of state and symbol of national unity, whereas the Chancellor is the head of government. The President is elected by the Federal Convention (a special assembly consisting of all members of the Bundestag and an equal number of delegates elected by the parliaments of the states) for a five-year term, which can be renewed once. Frank-Walter Steinmeier has been in office since March 2017.
