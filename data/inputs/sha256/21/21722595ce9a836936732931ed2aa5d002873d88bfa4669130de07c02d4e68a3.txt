As of March 2024, the President of Germany is Frank-Walter Steinmeier.

Key points about Frank-Walter Steinmeier:

1. He has been serving as the President of Germany since 19 March 2017.

2. He was re-elected for a second term on 13 February 2022.

3. Before becoming President, he served as Minister of Foreign Affairs from 2005 to 2009 and again from 2013 to 2017.

4. He was also Vice-Chancellor of Germany from 2007 to 2009.

5. Steinmeier is a member of the Social Democratic Party of Germany (SPD).

It's important to note that in Germany, the role of President is largely ceremonial, with the Chancellor serving as the head of government and holding most executive power. The current Chancellor of Germany is Olaf Scholz.
