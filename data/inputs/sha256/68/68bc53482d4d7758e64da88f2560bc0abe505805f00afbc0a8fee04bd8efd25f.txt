Here's a mnemonic for the Kanji meaning "wish" (希望 - kibō) using the primitives "clock" (時 - toki) and "heart" (心 - kokoro):

**"Your heart's desire ticks away like a clock, reminding you to make your wish before time runs out."**


Here's how it breaks down:

* **Clock (時 - toki):**  Represents the passing of time, emphasizing the urgency of making a wish.
* **Heart (心 - kokoro):** Represents your deepest desire or longing, which is the essence of a wish.


This mnemonic connects the primitives to the concept of a wish by highlighting the idea that time is precious and we should express our heart's desires before it's too late.
