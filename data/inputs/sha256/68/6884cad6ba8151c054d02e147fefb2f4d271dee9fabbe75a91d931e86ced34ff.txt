What a great approach to learning Kanji!

For the Kanji meaning "Wish" (, negai) with primitives clock and heart, here's a mnemonic:

**"Make a heartfelt wish at midnight, when time is ticking away for its fulfillment."**

Or, a more playful option:

**"Heart beats like a clock at midnight, wishing away for my dreams to come true."**

These mnemonics associate the given primitives (clock and heart) with a vivid, memorable scenario that helps you remember the Kanji and its meaning. Good luck with your Kanji learning journey!
