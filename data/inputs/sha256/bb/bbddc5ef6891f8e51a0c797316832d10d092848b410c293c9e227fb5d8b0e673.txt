everton manager roberto martinez was forced to defend another penalty fiasco at the club after ross barkley missed from the spot in their 1-0 win against burnley at goodison park . the untried barkley inexplicably took the 10th minute kick -- awarded for a foul by david jones on aaron lennon -- rather than leighton baines , who has scored 15 penalties from 16 attempts in the premier league . although there was no dispute between the team-mates this time , it brought back memories of everton 's match against west brom in january when kevin mirallas grabbed the ball from baines to take a penalty - and missed .
