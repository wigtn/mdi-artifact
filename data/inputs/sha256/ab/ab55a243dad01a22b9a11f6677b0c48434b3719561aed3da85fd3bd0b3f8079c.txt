Here's a quick, efficient, and adaptable office workout routine that can be completed in just 20 minutes, utilizing common office spaces and minimal equipment:

**"Office Blitz Workout" Routine (20 minutes)**

### Warm-up (2 minutes)
- Stand up from your chair and march in place or jump slightly while raising your arms. Quickly move to the desk and do some light arm circles (forward and backward).
- Do 10-15 desk push-ups or modified push-ups with your hands on the chair.

### Station 1: Chair Squats & Desk Dips (4 minutes)
- Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart near your chair.
- Do three sets of 12-15 chair squats.
- Place your hands on the edge of the chair and do three sets of 12-15 desk dips (lowering your body by bending your elbows until your arms are bent at a 90-degree angle).

### Station 2: Desk Push-Aways & Leg Lifts (4 minutes)
- Stand tall with your feet hip-width apart in front of your desk.
- Hold the edge of the desk with both hands shoulder-height and do three sets of 12-15 desk push-aways (slowly extend your arms straight, then pull back in).
- Lift one leg off the ground behind you, keeping it straight, and hold for a brief second before lowering it back down. Alternate legs for three sets of 12-15 reps per leg.

### Station 3: Desk Plank & Wall Sit (4 minutes)
- Move to a stable wall and assume a plank position with hands shoulder-width apart and feet hip-width apart, engaging your core.
- Hold the plank for as long as you can (aim for at least 30 seconds to start).
- After completing the plank, stand with your back against the wall and perform three sets of 60-second wall sits.

### Station 4: Quick Cardio & Stretching (6 minutes)
- Jump up and down in place or jog in a small circle around your desk for 30 seconds.
- Quickly move to a doorway or stable frame and do some lateral leg lifts, arm stretches, and neck stretches on both sides. Hold each stretch for 15-20 seconds.

### Cool-down (1 minute)
- End with some deep breathing exercises while seated or standing comfortably.

### Tips:
- Adjust the intensity and number of reps based on your fitness level.
- If you have limited space or need less movement, swap chair squats for seated leg lifts or seated leg raises.
- Try to maintain proper form throughout each exercise to avoid injury.
- Schedule this workout at least twice a day, ideally during breaks or lunch hours.

This efficient office workout will help you stay active, boost energy levels, and improve overall fitness despite a busy schedule.
